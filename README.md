I build **agentic AI systems** and ship them as products.

Most of my work now lives where AI meets regulation — domain agents for anti-money laundering, ESG, and compliance reporting, where a wrong answer is a legal problem rather than a bad demo. The rest is consumer software: voice interfaces, desktop apps, things people actually open every day.

I came up through control engineering and embedded firmware. That still shows: I care more about systems that behave predictably under load than about benchmarks.

## What I'm building

Alongside that, I'm starting **[Intuitive Evolution](https://intuitive-evolution-site.vercel.app)** — sustainable robotics, built on the idea that a robot should do more with less. Less material, less energy, fewer parts you can't repair. That constraint runs through the engineering, not just the marketing. Our first robot kit is **Alan**, and we open-sourced **[herm](https://github.com/jpalepu/herm_ws)**, the modular v1.0 that got us there. herm remains the most fun thing I've worked on.

I'm also the founder of **[ahsk](https://ahsk.app)** — a voice-first AI assistant for the Mac. Dictation under 250 ms, translation across 50+ languages, AI rewriting, all inside whatever app you're already in. And I co-created **[Cverra](https://cverra.com)**, which writes a resume against the actual job description instead of a template.

## What I work with

Most of my time goes to AI and agents: multi-agent orchestration, retrieval systems and graph RAG, evaluation harnesses, and the unglamorous plumbing that decides whether any of it survives production. Python for the models and the pipelines, TypeScript and Next.js for everything users touch, Swift and Kotlin when it has to be native.

Underneath that is a control engineering and embedded firmware background — ROS, MATLAB and Simulink, C and C++ down to ESP-IDF on bare boards. It's why I tend to trust systems that behave predictably under load more than ones that benchmark well, and it's most of the reason the robotics work feels like coming home.
